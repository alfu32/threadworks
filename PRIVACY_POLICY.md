# Privacy Policy

Last updated: September 19, 2026

Some functionalities would require a person to sign in using their google, github or microsoft credentials, the credentials are not stored, the user details are however stored in the files the useris working on on the user's own disk drive, the consequences of distributing those files fall entirely in the responsibility of the user.
